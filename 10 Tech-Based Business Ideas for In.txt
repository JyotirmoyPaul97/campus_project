♻️ Waste2Value — Smart Waste Management & Recycling Platform
1. Executive Summary

Waste2Value is a technology-driven waste management platform that aims to solve India's problems of poor waste segregation, inefficient waste collection, improper recycling, and excessive landfill usage.

The platform connects citizens, waste collectors, recycling centers, businesses, and municipalities through a single digital ecosystem. Its goal is simple: turn waste into a resource and create economic value from recycling.

2. Problem Statement

India generates a huge amount of solid waste every day, but several problems remain:

❌ Poor segregation of wet, dry and recyclable waste
❌ Inefficient waste collection routes
❌ Recyclable materials ending up in landfills
❌ Lack of awareness among citizens
❌ Informal waste workers lack digital coordination
❌ Recycling centers have difficulty getting consistent recyclable material
❌ Municipalities lack real-time waste-management data
3. Proposed Solution

Waste2Value provides a smart digital platform where users can:

Segregate → Schedule Collection → Collect → Sort → Recycle → Reward

The system uses AI, GPS, data analytics and digital payments to make the entire process more efficient.

5. How It Works
User separates wet, dry and recyclable waste.
User uploads a photo or selects the waste category.
AI helps identify the waste type.
User schedules a pickup.
Nearby collector receives the request.
Collector picks up and records the waste.
Recyclable waste is sent to an authorized recycler.
User receives points/rewards based on eligible recyclable material.
Platform generates data for businesses and municipalities.
